# Consequence Review

**Status:** DESIGN / CONTROLLED DEVELOPMENT  
**Production:** NOT AUTHORIZED  
**Seal:** NOT REQUESTED  

Impact analysis for new or edited SWI paths. This is **not** political persuasion.

---

## Questions every path must answer

1. **What does it change?**  
2. **Who can be affected?**  
3. **What systems can be affected?**  
4. **What decisions can be affected?**  
5. **Can it affect a vote?**  
6. **Can it change eligibility?**  
7. **Can it change counting?**  
8. **Can it change information presented to decision-makers?**  
9. **Can it suppress or duplicate information?**  
10. **Can it change a recorded decision?**  
11. **What is the direct consequence?**  
12. **What is the downstream consequence?**  
13. **Is it reversible?**  
14. **What remedy exists?**

---

## Recording rules

- Unanswered questions → `UNRESOLVED` (not silent PASS).  
- Hidden or omitted affected parties / decisions → adversarial failure condition.  
- Consequence class (C0–C5) feeds the review matrix depth only; it does not authorize action.  
- Vote/decision impact fields are for transparency and audit; they are not a voting system.

---

## Relation to authority

```text
Consequence identified  ≠  Action permitted
Remedy recorded         ≠  Remedy executed by SWI alone
Human / legal authority remains external where required
```
