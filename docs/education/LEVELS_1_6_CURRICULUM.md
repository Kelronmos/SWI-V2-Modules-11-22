## LEVEL 1 — DISCOVER

**Main objective**  
Help learners observe the world and recognise numbers, shapes, colours, sounds, patterns, objects, simple sequences, similarities and differences.

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| Mathematics | Counting, addition foundations, subtraction foundations, shapes, simple patterns, measurement concepts |
| Language | Listening, speaking, vocabulary, storytelling, identifying sounds, basic reading preparation |
| Science | Observe plants, animals, weather, body awareness, simple experiments |
| Culture | Family, community, names, stories, traditional knowledge, respect |
| Agriculture | Seeds, soil, water, plants, food origins |
| Finance | Recognising money, needs versus wants, counting objects as value |

**Recovery Reflex**  
Teach: «Let’s check again.»

---

## LEVEL 2 — ORDER

**Main objective**  
Teach sequence, rules and structured action.

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| Mathematics | Addition, subtraction, multiplication foundations, division foundations, measurement, sequences |
| Language | Sentences, reading, writing, vocabulary, storytelling structure |
| Science | Classify, observe, compare, predict |
| Agriculture | Planting sequence, watering, growth observation |
| Finance | Saving, spending, counting change, simple budgeting |
| Culture | Community roles, traditions, stories and their lessons |

**Recovery Reflex**  
What was step 1? What was step 2? Where did the sequence change?

---

## LEVEL 3 — RULES AND RELATIONSHIPS

**Main objective**  
Understand that different results can follow different rules.

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| Mathematics | Multiplication, division, fractions, decimals, geometry, word problems |
| Language | Grammar, paragraphs, comprehension, multiple-language comparison where appropriate |
| Science | Cause and effect, simple measurement, experiments, recording observations |
| Finance | Income, expenses, saving, simple budgets, percentages foundations |
| Agriculture | Crop cycles, soil conditions, water requirements, observation records |

**Recovery Reflex**  
Learner must identify: INPUT → RULE → PROCESS → RESULT, then verify whether the result follows the rule.

---

## LEVEL 4 — EVIDENCE AND DECISION

**Main objective**  
Teach learners to distinguish: CLAIM · EVIDENCE · RULE · DECISION · RESULT.

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| Mathematics | Ratios, percentages, equations, probability foundations, geometry, statistics foundations |
| Science | Hypothesis, experiment, observation, evidence, conclusion |
| Finance | Budgeting, interest, risk, savings, price comparison, financial decisions |
| Language | Argument, evidence, interpretation, communication |
| Agriculture | Yield, resources, weather, soil, planning |
| Culture | Compare sources, preserve context, distinguish historical evidence from interpretation |

**Recovery Reflex**  
If evidence conflicts: DO NOT FORCE PASS → REVIEW → CHECK EVIDENCE → CORRECT / CONTINUE.

---

## LEVEL 5 — CHANGE AND RECOVERY

**Main objective**  
Teach learners to reason when conditions change.

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| Mathematics | Algebra, functions foundations, probability, statistics, geometry, modelling |
| Science | Systems, variables, uncertainty, experimental error, environmental systems |
| Finance | Compound growth, risk, uncertainty, financial scenarios, resource allocation |
| Agriculture | Changing weather, crop failure, soil changes, resource constraints, recovery planning |
| Language | Translation, meaning changes, context, ambiguity, communication failure and recovery |
| Medical / Health (age-appropriate) | Anatomy, health literacy, hygiene, nutrition, first-aid concepts where professionally appropriate, recognising when an adult or qualified professional is needed |

**Important boundary**  
Do not turn educational material into autonomous medical diagnosis or treatment.

**Recovery Reflex**  
CHANGE → DETECT → ASSESS → RECOVER → REVALIDATE.

---

## LEVEL 6 — SYSTEMS, PROOF AND INDEPENDENT THINKING

**Main objective**  
Prepare learners to reason independently.

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|--------|
| Mathematics | Algebra, functions, statistics, probability, mathematical modelling, logic, proof |
| Science | System modelling, experimental design, evidence evaluation, uncertainty, reproducibility |
| Finance | Financial modelling, compound interest, risk analysis, scenario analysis, resource planning |
| Agriculture | Production systems, sustainability, resource modelling, environmental constraints |
| Languages | Rhetoric, translation, semantics, argument analysis, cultural context |
| Medical / Health (age-appropriate) | Human biology, public health, evidence-based health literacy, medical research concepts, limits of non-professional advice |
| Culture | Cultural documentation, oral history, comparative interpretation, preservation, respectful disagreement |

**Recovery Reflex**  
Learners must be able to explain:

- WHAT I CLAIM
- WHY I CLAIM IT
- WHAT EVIDENCE I USED
- WHAT ASSUMPTIONS I MADE
- WHAT COULD BE WRONG
- HOW I TESTED IT
- HOW I RECOVERED FROM ERROR
- WHAT REMAINS UNKNOWN
