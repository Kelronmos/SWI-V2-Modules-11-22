## 1. Purpose

This manual defines the architecture required for SWI to operate inside an organization where:

- the company constitution defines foundational organizational rules;
- company policies define approved operational rules;
- applicable law and regulation define external legal constraints;
- organizational structure defines positions and responsibilities;
- delegated authority defines who may make particular decisions;
- business posts/modules contain relevant information and workflows;
- Human Authority makes authorized organizational decisions;
- Execution performs only actions that have already received valid authorization.

SWI is intended to make organizational decision processes structured, inspectable, evidence-aware and bounded.

SWI does not become the employer, manager, director, legal authority or decision-maker.

## 5. Company Constitution

The company constitution is treated as a foundational organizational source.

It may define:

- governance structure;
- offices and positions;
- decision-making powers;
- board or executive responsibilities;
- delegation mechanisms;
- organizational objectives;
- internal governance constraints;
- escalation mechanisms.

SWI must preserve provenance and version information.

A changed constitution must not silently replace the previous record.

```text

     ↓

     ↓
VERIFY

        ↓
```


## 19. Harm of Acting vs Harm of Waiting



SWI is not designed to replace the organization.

It should reveal:

```text

ASSUMPTIONS
EVIDENCE
UNKNOWN INFORMATION
AUTHORITY
BOUNDARIES
CONFLICTS
PATTERNS
RISKS
HARM
CONSEQUENCES
LIMITATIONS
```

The system can say:

- "This is what the evidence shows."
- "This is the rule that applies."
- "This is the authority that exists."
- "This authority has expired."
- "This information is outside scope."
- "This pattern requires review."
- "This action creates unacceptable downstream risk."
- "This path must halt."

But the final human question remains:

> WHO HAS THE AUTHORITY TO DECIDE?

And the answer must never silently become:

> SWI.

The governing principle is:

```text
EVIDENCE BEFORE CLAIM
BOUNDARY BEFORE ACCESS
AUTHORITY BEFORE ACTION
HUMANITY BEFORE AUTOMATION
```
