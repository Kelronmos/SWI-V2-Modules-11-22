# SWI Document Discipline & Confidentiality Boundary

**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Status:** RESEARCH / EXPERIMENTAL  
**Production:** NOT AUTHORIZED

---

## Hard rule

1. **Confidential government / institutional proposals are never committed** to public SWI repositories.
2. The Botswana Government Digital Provenance & Authenticity Architecture PDF (and any similar classified or submitted proposals) remain **outside** the repository tree.
3. Only reusable, non-confidential research principles that already align with SWI evidence law may be recorded.

---

## What may be recorded

- Generic architectural principles already public or independently derived (e.g. separation of identity, integrity, and provenance; registry ≠ document store; evidence before claim).
- Controlled experimental protocols (path-lock, 8× repeatability, seal gates).
- Status tables, limitation statements, and honest non-claims.

## What must never be recorded

- Full text or substantial excerpts of confidential government proposals.
- Institution-specific operational details not already public.
- Any material that would imply production authorization or real-world deployment.

---

## Alignment note (high-level only)

Independent of any confidential proposal, SWI research continues to treat:

- Identity ≠ Integrity ≠ Provenance
- Evidence ≠ Authorization
- Authorization ≠ Action
- TESTED ≠ SEALED ≠ PRODUCTION AUTHORIZED

These distinctions remain internal research discipline. They do not constitute a claim about any external architecture or national system.

---

## Archive practice

Local working copies of confidential material (if any) stay in the operator’s private workspace and are never pushed.

Repository updates for research protocols use the ordinary evidence chain:

CLAIM → IMPLEMENTATION → TEST → RESULT → LIMITATION → AUDIT

with explicit NOT AUTHORIZED / NOT SEALED status where applicable.
