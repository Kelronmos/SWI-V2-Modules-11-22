This document defines a **hard constitutional boundary** of SWI.  
It is not a security rule. It is a prohibition on SWI becoming the machinery of human domination, displacement, or existential control.

---

## 1. Purpose

> The more irreversible the consequence, the higher the human authority required — and some actions remain outside SWI's autonomous execution boundary altogether.

This is not about making SWI powerful enough to control civilization.  
It is about making sure SWI can never quietly become the machinery that does.
